# References

## Video Reference — Quantum Key Distribution

**Source:** [YouTube Video](https://youtu.be/V3WzH2up7Os?si=YnTROu_erZgkphxl)

**Topic:** Quantum Key Distribution (QKD)

### Summary

This video provides an introduction to **Quantum Key Distribution (QKD)** and explains how quantum mechanics can be used to securely distribute cryptographic keys. It introduces the **One-Time Pad**, the motivation for quantum-based key distribution, and the **BB84 protocol** using photon polarization and randomly chosen measurement bases. It also discusses the **E91 protocol**, which uses quantum entanglement, and explains how QKD can detect eavesdropping because quantum measurements disturb quantum states. Finally, the video briefly discusses practical QKD implementations through **fiber-optic and satellite communication** and its potential role in a future Quantum Internet.

### Key Topics Covered

- One-Time Pad and secure key distribution
- Limitations of classical public-key cryptography
- Quantum Key Distribution (QKD)
- BB84 protocol
- Photon polarization
- Bits and measurement bases
- No-cloning theorem
- E91 protocol
- Quantum entanglement
- Eavesdropping detection
- Fiber-optic QKD
- Satellite-based QKD
- Quantum Internet

### Relevance to This Research

This source was used as an **introductory reference for understanding Quantum Key Distribution and the BB84 protocol** before studying the original BB84 paper by Bennett and Brassard (1984). The video provides an accessible overview of the concepts and terminology used in BB84, which helped establish the background needed to understand the original research paper.
