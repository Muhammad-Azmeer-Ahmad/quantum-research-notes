# 🔬 Quantum Research Notes

> A structured, ongoing research log — paper analyses and implementations in quantum information, cryptography, and networks.

---

## 📖 Purpose

This repo documents my progression through foundational and current research in quantum information science, following a deliberate path rather than a random paper collection.

Each entry includes a full breakdown of the paper — problem, method, math, results — along with my own understanding, a partial reproduction in code, and open questions I haven't resolved yet.

**Approach:** `Read` → `Understand` → `Reproduce` → `Extend`

Building toward research in quantum-secure networks and systems.

## 🧭 Notes on Approach

Every paper folder follows a fixed template:

**Citation → Problem → Background → Method → Mathematics → Results → My Understanding → Reproduction → My Results → Limitations/Questions**

The last section is intentional — this is a learning log, not a claim of original research. I try to be explicit about what I don't yet understand.

Original work by the paper's authors is always clearly separated from my own analysis, reproduction, and results.
